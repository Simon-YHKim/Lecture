## Line 4 — What a technician actually does with this (Frame 4)

**Time:** 2:50–3:55

    (1 card) [light] First, proposing a jig. [calm] Holding the same work by hand every time takes longer and comes out differently for each person. [calm] You draw the jig that would make the job easier and propose it. [calm] Asking in words and handing over a drawing are not the same thing. [light] The drawing carries dimensions. [calm] The person making it does not have to come back and ask.

    (2 card) [light] Second, checking a layout. [calm] You check where equipment will stand on a drawing first. [calm] You look at whether a walkway is left and whether the door still opens. [calm] By then you cannot move it.

    (3 card) [pointing] Third, sourcing a spare part in a hurry. [calm] A machine is down and you need one part fast. [calm] First you find the part in the assembly drawing. [calm] Then you check the spare part drawing and talk to the machine shop. [calm] If you cannot read the drawing, this stops here. [calm] Reading and drawing exist so that this conversation is exact.

## Line 5 — Commands you reach for on the floor (Frame 5)

**Time:** 3:55–5:21

    [calm] There are six commands in the table on screen. [calm] Five of them you already typed in the drawing lessons. [calm] The same command lands in a different place on the floor.

    (1 row) [measured] Polyline, `PL`. [calm] It draws connected segments as one object. [calm] Whether to keep separate lines or bind them into a polyline follows the editing you plan. [calm] On a layout you run one around the machine footprint. [calm] Then the area comes out in one go, and it follows along when you move it.

    (2 row) [measured] Offset, `O`. [calm] It makes the same shape a set distance away. [calm] You get wall thickness and walkway clearance without picking a centre again.

    (3 row) [measured] Distance, `DI`. [calm] It measures between two points. [calm] You check on the spot whether the machine will fit. [calm] It leaves nothing behind on the drawing.

    (4 row) [measured] List, `LI`. [calm] It shows what a selected object is. [calm] On a drawing you received you check whether that line really is the length it looks. [calm] Two lines can look the same and hold different values.

    (5 row) [measured] Match properties, `MA`. [calm] It carries properties over to another object. [calm] Drawings from other people come with mixed layers. [calm] You send them back where they belong without redrawing.

    (6 row) [measured] Plot, `PLOT`. [calm] It sends the drawing to paper or PDF. [cautionary] When you talk to the machine shop you do not show them a screen. [light] You hand them paper. [calm] You check the scale before it goes out.

    [cautionary] You do not have to memorise the six today. [light] Remember the situations. [calm] Stand in that spot and the command comes back.
